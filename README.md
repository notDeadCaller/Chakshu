# Chakshu (The-All-Seeing-Eye) 
### Chakshu is a personal safety Android app that records, timestamps, and preserves evidence in a way no authority can suppress. <br>
When triggered silently via a volume button sequence, Chakshu starts recording audio, captures your GPS location, and immediately blasts an SOS to 
your trusted contacts via SMS — no internet required, no unlocking needed, no visible indication to anyone around you. <br>
Every audio chunk is SHA-256 hashed, anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps, and stored on IPFS — a decentralized network with no single
server to subpoena, no company to pressure, and no kill switch. A public dashboard publishes a tamper-proof incident record the moment recording begins. 
The evidence exists before anyone can decide it shouldn't. <br>
Every existing safety app trusts someone — a government server, a private company, the police — to keep your evidence safe. Chakshu trusts no one. 
The math keeps it safe instead.<br>
Built for women and anyone who has ever felt unsafe and known that feeling alone wasn't enough.
